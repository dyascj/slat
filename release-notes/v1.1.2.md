Rules that stick, and steadier focus.

- **Rule changes apply right away.** Setting an app to always float now floats its windows that are already open, not just new ones. Removing the rule tiles them again. Windows you floated or tiled by hand keep your choice.
- **Restarting keeps your rules.** A window whose app is set to float no longer comes back tiled after Slat restarts.
- **Width rules leave other apps alone.** Giving one app a fixed width no longer stops every other app from opening at your default width.
- **Focus changes no longer stutter.** Moving focus to another app's window used to wait on that app partway through the slide. With focus follows mouse, the row could also jump to the app's previous window and back. Both are fixed.
