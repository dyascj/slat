Focus moves land on the first press again.

- **Moving focus no longer takes two presses.** Since 1.1.4, Chrome, Zen, Slack and other browser-based apps could slip a hidden helper window into the row each time they took focus. Moving focus landed on that invisible window first, so it took a second press to reach the next real one. Slat ignores those helper windows again, like it did in 1.1.3.
