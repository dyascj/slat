Smoother swipes, a lighter engine, and more of it in Settings.

- **Swipes** keep moving when they're slow or uneven, and the first window snaps flush to the edge when you stop.
- **Glide.** Settings › Gestures sets how far the row coasts after you lift your fingers, and can stop the row at the first and last window.
- **Up and down reach the next row.** With workspaces on, mod + `J`/`K` (or the arrows) move within a stack, then on to the workspace below or above.
- **Balance widths** with mod + `=`: every column takes the focused window's width.
- **Focus border** in a choice of colors, and a **Dim strength** slider, under Layout › Feel.
- **Resize with the pointer:** hold a key combination and move the pointer near a window's edge.
- **Animate switching** between workspaces, if you'd rather see the rows slide.
- The row reflows live while a window resizes, re-centers when you toggle full width, and windows at startup are sorted before they're tiled.
- The menu bar's workspace number changes with the switch instead of a second later.
- Animation is paced to your display, including 120 Hz screens, and the engine idles more quietly: it stops waking for pointer moves nothing uses, for focus changes inside the same window, and for full-screen redraws of the dim overlay.
